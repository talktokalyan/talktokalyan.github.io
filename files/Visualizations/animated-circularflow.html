4-Sector Open Economy

Aggregate Demand / Output (Y)

Y = C + I + G + (X - M)
National Output equals total spending across Consumption, Investment, Government Purchases, and Net Exports.

Leakages & Injections Equilibrium

S + T + M = I + G + X + TR
Equilibrium holds when total economy Leakages (S + T + M) match total Injections (I + G + X + TR).

Disposable Income (Yd)

Yd = Y - T + TR
Income available to Households after paying Direct Taxes (T) to Government and receiving Transfers (TR).
